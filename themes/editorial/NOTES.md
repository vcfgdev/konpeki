# Editorial theme

Warm paper and near-black ink keep the palette quiet; deep rust marks titles and code. Square corners, hairline rules, and IBM Plex Serif display roles evoke restrained print without prescribing composition. Plex Sans remains the reading face, with Plex Mono for code.

IBM Plex family reference: https://www.ibm.com/plex/

The IBM Plex Serif Latin 400 and 600 WOFF2 files and `fonts/plex-serif-OFL.txt` come from the pinned npm package `@fontsource/ibm-plex-serif@5.3.0`: https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource/ibm-plex-serif/v/5.3.0

The Sans and Mono files are copies of the common bundled Fontsource 5.3.0 files,
with their original `OFL.txt` and `plex-mono-OFL.txt` licenses.
