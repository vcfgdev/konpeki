# Dark theme notes

Neutral charcoal keeps the canvas quiet while luminous cyan carries titles and key signals. A pale contrast field reverses the hierarchy without introducing navy; crisp rules and square corners keep the treatment editorial rather than panel-heavy.

IBM Plex Sans (normal 400/600/700 and italic 400) and IBM Plex Mono (normal 400) are bundled from Fontsource 5.3.0 under the SIL Open Font License; see `fonts/OFL.txt` and `fonts/plex-mono-OFL.txt`.
