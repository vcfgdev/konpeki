# Dense Data

A restrained, square-edged technical-report treatment: neutral paper, compact rhythm, dark ink, and slate-blue hierarchy. Tabular lining numerals support aligned quantitative reading without prescribing table composition.

IBM Plex Sans (Latin 400, italic 400, 600, and 700) and IBM Plex Mono (Latin 400) are bundled from Fontsource 5.3.0 under the SIL Open Font License; license texts are in `fonts/`.
