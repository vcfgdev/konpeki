# Résumé

Use for a résumé or CV, even when it is a single page. Apply the
[shared guidance](../SKILL.md#before-authoring) and the
[authoring floor](../floor.md); do not treat it as a marketing slide or a generic card.

- Start with the theme's portrait `a4` preset; use Letter or another size when
  the destination calls for it. Let relevant supplied experience determine
  length rather than assuming every résumé must fit one page.
- Make the person's name, contact details, and relevant experience easy to scan.
  Choose section order for the candidate and role; do not manufacture a summary,
  project, award, or skills section merely to fill a template.
- Keep organization, role, dates, and location unambiguous. Use consistent date
  formatting and clear chronology; align dates without detaching them from the
  role they describe.
- Write concise evidence-led accomplishments from supplied facts. Never invent
  metrics, seniority, qualifications, or causal claims. Preserve materially
  different roles rather than merging them to make the layout cleaner.
- Start with a simple reading order and flowing text. Use additional columns
  only when they remain readable and extract in the intended order. Avoid
  skill-rating bars and icon-only contact information.
- Keep a role heading with its first accomplishment. Prefer breaks between
  roles or sections; if an entry must continue, make that continuation clear.
  Remove low-value repetition before reducing type to meet a page limit.

Inspect the PDF at normal reading size. Check contact text, links, dates,
selectable text, and extraction order. Do not claim ATS compatibility from
appearance or a clean overflow report alone.
