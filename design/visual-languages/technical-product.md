# Technical product

Use for product explanations, processes and comparisons where a concrete visual
can carry the argument. This is art direction, not a palette or a fixed template.

- Give the product/interface/output enough space to explain the claim.
- Apply the selected mode's framing and emphasis rules; do not flatten
  unrelated content into equally prominent boxes.
- Connect a process to its tangible output. Keep labels near their connectors.
- Make selection/recommendation distinct without obscuring alternatives.
- Keep diagrams and annotations readable at the intended viewing size. Shared
  card headers round outer top corners only, not the internal bottom seam.
- Avoid importing website density, tiny gray labels, ubiquitous registration
  marks, or scroll effects into slides.

Works with Blue–cyan light or Graphite dark; these are examples, not limits.
