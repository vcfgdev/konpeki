Make a three-page presentation helping the operations lead at Moss choose a
support inbox for a six-person team. Compare the options against the team's
actual needs and recommend what to trial.

---

Moss and all three products are fictional. The team needs email intake, shared
assignment and a complete CSV export of ticket history. Its monthly subscription
budget is $150. Chat support would be useful later, but is not required now.
The team does not have capacity to operate its own server.

- Postbox: hosted, $18 per agent per month. Email intake, shared assignment and
  complete CSV history export are included. No chat channel. No setup fee.
- Threadline: hosted, $24 per agent per month. Includes email, chat, shared
  assignment and complete CSV history export. No setup fee.
- OwnDesk: self-hosted, no software subscription fee. Includes email, chat,
  shared assignment and complete CSV history export. The team must supply
  hosting, backups, upgrades and incident response; their costs are unknown.

These are hand-authored scenario specifications, not vendor research. Hosted
prices exclude tax and assume six paid agents. There are no measured usability,
reliability or migration results. A feature being listed does not establish
how well it works.

---

Recommend a first trial, explain what would make the other hosted option worth
its extra cost, and identify what to verify before purchase. Treat self-hosting
as an operational commitment rather than a zero-cost option.
