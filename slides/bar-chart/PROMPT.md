---

Birch is a fictional internal operations team. These are synthetic, hand-authored
median active processing times per completed case. Waiting time is excluded.
Each workflow has 80 completed cases in the before period and 80 different
completed cases in the after period. Lower is better.

Workflow | Before median (minutes) | After median (minutes)
Account setup | 18 | 12
Address correction | 11 | 10
Refund review | 25 | 19
Access removal | 7 | 8
Supplier onboarding | 32 | 24

The before and after periods each lasted two weeks. A revised work queue was
introduced between them. Cases were not randomized or matched, and case
complexity and staffing could differ. These are independent groups, not paired
measurements of the same cases. No raw times, variability estimates or quality
measurements are supplied.

---

Four workflows have lower observed medians; access removal rises from 7 to 8
minutes. Supplier onboarding has the largest absolute median reduction, eight
minutes, but still has the highest after-period median at 24 minutes.

Recommend investigating the access-removal increase and checking case mix and
quality before extending the revised queue. The data do not establish that the
queue caused any change. Do not multiply median differences by case counts to
claim total time or money saved, or imply that a lower median means every case
became faster.
